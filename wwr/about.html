Rapid Ridge Rafting

Our mission is to deliver safe, unforgettable river adventures built on teamwork, respect for nature, and excitement on the water.

History

Started by passionate outdoor guides, Rapid Ridge Rafting grew from a single raft into a trusted adventure team focused on safety and memorable river trips.

Over the years, we have expanded our guided routes while protecting river habitats and helping first-time paddlers and seasoned adventurers build confidence on the water.
